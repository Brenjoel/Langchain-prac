In langchain , if we add a python functions(which are not runnable and does not have invoke function),
then the langchain automatically converts them into runnable lambda functions
ex: retriever | formatdocs | prompt_template is converted into
    retriever | RunnableLambda(formatdocs) | prompt_template

by default the outputs from format_docs  (context and query)  are not sent to the prompt_template directly
